I see the world 36 frames at a time.

Give me a camera and I'll notice the light on a wall you've walked past a hundred times. Every frame is a choice about what matters and what gets cropped out. Photography keeps my eyes fresh.

The gallery show · Mews Art Gallery

I curated 35+ works for the show, representing one year of work from our film club, a group of practicing photographers still learning to call themselves artists. Each month brought a new theme and film stock, from double exposures and diptychs to strangers and maximalism. Visitors lounged in our recreated living room while our photo archive played on an old TV. Framed prints sold for $50 each, and we raised $1,000+ for Wide Rainbow, an after-school art program in under-resourced neighborhoods.
