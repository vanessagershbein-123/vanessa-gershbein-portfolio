Pricing & packaging

PMMTrack 5

What to charge, what goes in each plan, and how to change prices without a revolt.

Price is the fastest lever a company has, and the one most teams touch least often. Getting it right starts with what a product is worth to the people buying it. Then comes the question of what goes in each plan. Eventually you face the hardest conversation in marketing: telling customers the price is going up.
