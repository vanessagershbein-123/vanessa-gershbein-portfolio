Redesign the plans

PMMDrill 5.212 minIntermediate

Skills: Pricing & promotion

The prompt

The facts.

  • In April 2024, Salesforce made Slack AI available to every paid Slack customer as a paid add-on, at $10 per user a month on the Pro and Business+ plans. It included search answers with citations, channel and thread summaries, and a daily recap of channels a user follows. Salesforce said customers were saving an average of 97 minutes per user a week, based on its own internal analysis. It also cited Slack research showing that while 94% of executives called AI an urgent priority, only one in four desk workers had tried AI tools at work1.
  • AI features cost money to run every time someone uses them.

Your task: It's early 2025. Leadership wants far more Slack customers using AI, without simply giving away what makes it valuable. Redesign the plans: which AI features belong in which plan, what happens to the $10 add-on and whether any prices change. Defend your choices.

Your earlier answer

Sample answer

Put a taste of AI in every paid plan and the heavier features in the plans above it.

  • Every paid plan: summaries. They're easy to understand, useful on day one and build the habit, which is the real problem when only one in four workers has even tried AI.
  • Business+: the features bigger teams care about most, such as search across everything, daily recaps and workflow automation. Raise the plan's price, but by less than the $10 add-on, so a customer who'd have bought both pays less than before.
  • A new top plan: company-wide search and governance for the largest customers, sold through sales.
  • The add-on: stop selling it, and let current buyers keep it until renewal so nobody loses features mid-contract.

What Slack actually did

On June 17, 2025, in its first pricing change since 2022, Slack folded AI into its paid plans. Pro kept its price and gained core AI features. Business+ rose from $12.50 to $15 per user a month on annual billing ($15 to $18 on monthly billing) and gained advanced AI. A new Enterprise+ plan added enterprise search2.

The $10 add-on stopped being sold on Slack's website, and customers who had it kept it until their first renewal after August 17, 2025. Summaries and huddle notes now come with every paid plan. Search, recaps, translations, file summaries and workflow automation come with Business+ and Enterprise+, and enterprise search is Enterprise+ only3.

Why it works

  • AI becomes a reason to pick a plan, not a separate purchase most customers skip.
  • The math favors AI buyers. The $2.50 Business+ increase is a quarter of the old add-on price, so customers who wanted AI pay less, while everyone on the plan pays a little more.
  • Summaries in Pro go after the adoption problem with the easiest feature to try.
  • The costliest feature gets its own price. Search across a whole company's tools lives in the new top plan.

What interviewers push on

  • "Business+ customers who never wanted AI now pay 20% more. Won't some leave?" Some may step down to Pro, which is why Pro got core AI and kept its price.
  • "Why not make all AI free everywhere?" Running it isn't free, and the advanced features are what justify the higher plans.

Practice twist

Your company sells a design tool with Starter, Pro and Enterprise plans, and it's launching an AI image generator that's expensive to run. Which plan gets it, and do you meter it?
