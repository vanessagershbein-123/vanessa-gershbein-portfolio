Growth & campaigns

PMMTrack 7

Where growth comes from after launch, and how to plan a campaign that moves a real number.

Launch day gets the attention, but most of a product's growth happens afterward, one stage of the customer's journey at a time. Some growth compounds, with each customer bringing in the next. Some has to be bought, campaign by campaign. The hard part is knowing which is which, and proving what your spending actually caused.
