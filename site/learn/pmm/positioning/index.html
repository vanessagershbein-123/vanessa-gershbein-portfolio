Positioning & messaging

PMMTrack 3

Decide what you are, for whom and against what. Then say it clearly, to the right person at the right moment, and test it.

Positioning and messaging together are the most-owned job in product marketing: 91% of PMMs in PMA's 2025 survey own them Sourced. Messaging is how positioning turns into words. The work goes in that order: alternatives, differences, the map, the statement, the message, the test.
