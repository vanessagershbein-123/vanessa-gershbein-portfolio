Customer & market insight

PMMTrack 2

Who to target and what they actually need, from segments to jobs to be done.

Positioning only works once you know whom you're trying to win and what they actually need. That comes down to four skills: dividing a market and picking where to start, researching the people who buy and use, running interviews that reveal what customers are trying to get done, and turning all of it into an insight a team can act on.
