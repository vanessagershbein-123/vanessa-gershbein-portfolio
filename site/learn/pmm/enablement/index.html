Sales enablement, competitive intel & customer proof

PMMTrack 6

Give sales what they need to win, know the competition, and prove it with customers.

A product can be positioned perfectly and still lose the deal in a 30-minute call. Sales is where marketing gets tested live, by a buyer who's also talking to your rivals. The work here is giving reps what they need in that moment, knowing the competition cold, and backing every claim with customers buyers already believe.
