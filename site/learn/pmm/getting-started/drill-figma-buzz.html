Who owned what at the Figma Buzz launch?

PMMDrill 1.16 minBeginner

Skills: Interviews & career

The prompt

The launch. At its Config conference on May 7, 2025, Figma announced Figma Buzz, designed for brand and marketing teams1. That's a different user from the designers Figma is best known for. Figma launched Buzz in open beta, with templates, brand components, AI image tools and bulk asset creation2.

Your task, in about 150 words:

  1. Name one launch decision the PM likely owned, one the PMM owned, and one the brand team owned.
  2. Which internal team did the PMM need to win over first, and why?

You don't know how Figma actually split the work, so reason from the roles.

Your earlier answer

Sample answer

A sample answer, not a description of how Figma actually split the work.

  • PM: what ships in the open beta, such as whether AI image tools come on day one.
  • PMM: who Buzz is for first and how to frame it. A strong pick is marketers at companies that already pay for Figma, because designers there can set up the templates once and marketers can then make on-brand assets without waiting on design. That becomes the message. The PMM also sets an adoption goal, such as the share of customer teams that create a Buzz file in 30 days.
  • Brand: how Buzz looks and sounds as part of Figma.
  • Win over first: the account teams for existing Figma customers. Buzz spreads fastest inside companies already on Figma, and those teams own the relationships. If they can't pitch it to marketers, it never reaches the buyer, however good the product is.

Why it works

  • It keeps the three roles distinct: the PM owns scope, the PMM owns audience and message, and brand owns identity.
  • It picks one segment instead of "all marketers," and gives a reason grounded in the launch facts.
  • It names a measurable adoption goal, because the launch isn't the end goal.

What interviewers push on

  • "Why existing customers and not new ones?" Be ready to argue the trade-off: existing customers convert faster, but new logos make Buzz bigger.
  • "What's the main competitive alternative for this marketer?" Today it might be a template-based design tool such as Canva, or asking a designer for every asset.
  • "How would you know the launch worked by day 90?"

Practice twist

Same launch, but Figma has no sales or account team, and every user signs up on their own. Which internal team do you win over first now?
