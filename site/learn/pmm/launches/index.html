Go-to-market & launches

PMMTrack 4

Plan the launch, pick the motion, then make sure people actually adopt the thing.

A launch is where positioning meets real buyers. It takes a plan for how customers will find and buy the product, a sense of how big each release should be, goals that predict success, a push to make sure people actually use what shipped and, for AI products, extra work to win their trust.
