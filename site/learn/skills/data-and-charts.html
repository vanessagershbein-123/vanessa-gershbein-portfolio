Data & charts

Skill module8 lessons · 5 drills

Read any business chart out loud, pick the right chart for a question and turn the numbers into one clear point.

Data & charts is the skill of reading a chart out loud, picking the right chart for a question and turning numbers into one clear point. Marketing, brand and product jobs run on it: sales reviews, category data, survey results, launch dashboards.

Interviews test it head-on. McKinsey's own practice case hands you a chart and asks what observations you can derive from it, with a hint to look for trends that aren't obvious at first glance1. BCG tells candidates that a case means structuring an approach, asking questions, analyzing data and doing quick calculations2. Business schools train for it too: a consulting bootcamp run by Dartmouth's Tuck School of Business practices real interview prompts on exhibit interpretation and case math3.

The lessons build in order: read a chart, put numbers on a fair footing, pick a chart from the question, then the chart families, and last the charts that mislead and the one chart that makes your case. A one-page cheat sheet closes the lessons. Every lesson chart uses real public data. The five drills follow one made-up company, Pennywhistle Cold Brew, a cold-brew coffee brand sold in grocery stores, club stores and its own subscription app; every drill number is invented for practice, and each lesson's self-check gives you a first taste of it.
