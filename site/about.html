The short version of my story

I studied Communication Studies at Northeastern, with a minor in Environmental Studies and Sustainable Business Practices.

Then I spent six years as the person brands called when they needed to understand their customers: first at TapResearch, then Kantar, then Swayable. Two startups and one global consulting firm. The startups taught me to build from scratch and move fast. Kantar taught me rigor, and how to turn complex data into recommendations senior leaders could act on. Either way, I handed marketing leaders the insight, and they decided what to do with it.

At some point I realized I wanted to be the one making the call. So now I'm at McCombs as a Hildebrand Scholar, Forté Fellow, MIINT Fellow, and Marketing Fellow, getting ready to do exactly that.
